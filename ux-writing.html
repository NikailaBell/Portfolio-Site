UX Writing Projects

ITLS Content Strategy Information Architecture

A comprehensive deep dive into how clear, concise microcopy impacts user behavior and accessibility in digital platforms. This case study breaks down the end-to-end process from initial documentation to client delivery.

1. Written How-To Guide

The foundation of the project required translating complex, technical processes into plain, accessible language. The goal was to reduce cognitive load while ensuring the user felt guided and supported at every step.

The Process

  • Audited the existing technical documentation for jargon and friction points.
  • Restructured the information architecture to follow a logical, user-centric flow.
  • Established a consistent voice and tone that balanced authority with empathy.
Read the Guide Task Analysis

2. Demo How-To Guide

Words are only as effective as the interface they live in. I created a visual, interactive demonstration to show exactly how the written instructions translate into the live user interface.

Visual Storytelling

  • Integrated the microcopy directly into high-fidelity Figma mockups.
  • Created tooltips, error states, and success messages to guide users through the happy path and edge cases.
View Final Demo View Rough Sketch

3. Design Justification Paper

Every word choice on an interface is a design decision. This paper defends the rationale behind the microcopy, grounded in instructional design principles and UX best practices.

Key Arguments

  • Accessibility: Why specific vocabulary was chosen to meet reading-level standards.
  • Cognitive Load: The psychological reasoning behind breaking long instructions into progressive disclosures.
  • Before & After: Side-by-side comparisons of the original text versus the optimized microcopy.
Read the Justification

4. Client Presentation

The final phase involved synthesizing the research, documentation, and designs into a compelling narrative for stakeholders. It is crucial to communicate not just what was changed, but the business value of why it matters.

Final Deliverables

  • A strategic slide deck highlighting the core UX problems and the written solutions.
  • A live walk-through of the demo.
  • A handoff guide for the development team to implement the new copy.
