Get To Know Me

My Journey

I recently graduated from Weber State University with a Bachelor’s in Applied Mathematics and minors in Computer Science and UX/UI Design. Currently, I am expanding my horizons in the Instructional Technology and Learning Sciences Master’s program at Utah State University.

My Design Philosophy

While my foundation is deeply rooted in logic and data, my true passion is visual design. I love the challenge of taking complex, data-heavy systems and translating them into beautiful, intuitive interfaces. For me, Figma isn't just a wireframing tool—it's a canvas where I get to experiment with typography, layout, and visual storytelling. My goal is to bridge the gap between art and analytics, ensuring that every digital experience I craft is just as aesthetically engaging as it is technically accessible.

When I'm Not Designing...

You can usually find me playing pickleball, or working on creative outlets like drawing, painting, and sewing.
